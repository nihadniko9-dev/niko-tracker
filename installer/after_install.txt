Installed:
  - The tracking engine in WSL2 (distribution "NikoEngine"), with the models it downloaded.
  - The Blender add-on, in every Blender found on this computer and switched on.
    Open Blender: the "Niko track" workspace tab (or press N in the 3D view, tab "Niko").

If SAM 3 was skipped (no Hugging Face token), run "Download the models again" from the Start menu
after accepting Meta's license at https://huggingface.co/facebook/sam3.1.

Updates: press the round arrow at the top of the Niko tab, then restart Blender. It installs the
newest add-on and engine code from github.com/nihadniko9-dev/niko-tracker.
